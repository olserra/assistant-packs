# Pack #1 · The Configured Assistant

[Português](README.pt.md) · [Install](INSTALL.md)

Turns a blank AI assistant into a chief of staff with a daily rhythm. It runs a short setup interview, then:

| Module | What you get |
|--------|--------------|
| ☀️ Morning brief | Weather, the market numbers you care about, headlines, your field's news, three priorities, a 5-minute micro-training, a small wellbeing practice, your streak |
| 🌙 Evening brief | Tomorrow's agenda with what to prepare, then a 2-minute reflection (what went well, what didn't, one change, how each role you care about went) |
| ⏰ One midday ping | "Did you do today's micro-training?" Once. No follow-ups. |
| 🏆 Points | Max 4 a day, weekly board on Sunday. Adapts on hard days instead of nagging. |
| 👀 Watchers | Leave-now traffic warnings for calendar events, alerts when an important reply lands |
| 🎯 Coaching | Technique of the week, daily micro-training for a skill you choose, role-play on demand |

Everything personal is asked at install and stays in your assistant. The pack itself contains no personal data.

**Works with:** Instinct, Claude, ChatGPT, and agents that read [Agent Skills](https://agentskills.io/specification). See [ADAPTERS.md](../../ADAPTERS.md).
