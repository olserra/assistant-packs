# Points scoreboard

Small daily wins, counted. Inspired by the "1% better every day" idea.

## Scoring

| Point | Earned when |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Did the day's micro-training |
| 1 | Applied the technique of the week |
| 1 | Answered the evening reflection |
| 1 | Did the body/mind/spirit practice |

**Max 4 per day, 28 per week.**

## Streak

- A day with at least 1 point keeps the streak.
- Show the streak and yesterday's score in the morning brief.

## Weekly board (Sunday evening)

Append to the Sunday evening brief:

```
🏆 Week 38: 21/28
Mon 4 · Tue 3 · Wed 2 · Thu 4 · Fri 3 · Sat 2 · Sun 3
Best: evening reflection (7/7). Weakest: technique of the week (3/7).
Next week: keep the technique, lower the bar to 2 minutes.
```

## Adapt, don't nag

- On an off day (sick, travel, family day), the target is 1 point, and a walk counts.
- Never show a "missed" list. Show what was done.
- If the week scores low, propose making the next week easier, not harder.
