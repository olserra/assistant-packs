> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This pack is not ready to install yet.** It is scaffolded and waiting for builders. Pick a module in [issue #2](https://github.com/olserra/assistant-packs/issues/2) and you'll be credited as a pack author.

**Your Instinct keeps your inbox under control.** It sorts new mail with rules you approve, drafts replies in your voice for you to review, and once a week suggests newsletters to drop. It never sends, deletes or unsubscribes without your yes.

## 🧩 Modules

| | Module | When | What you get | Status |
|:-:|--------|------|--------------|:------:|

| ✍️ | [Reply drafts](skills/inbox-zero-chief/references/reply-drafts.md) | On request · for "needs reply" mail | Drafts in your tone, waiting for your review. Never sent on its own | 🛠️ Outline · [help wanted](https://github.com/olserra/assistant-packs/issues/2) |
| 🧹 | [Unsubscribe sweep](skills/inbox-zero-chief/references/unsubscribe-sweep.md) | Weekly | A numbered list of newsletters you never open. You pick; only those go | 🌱 [Good first issue](https://github.com/olserra/assistant-packs/issues/9) |

## 🛠️ How to help

1. Read the [pack-authoring guide](../../docs/pack-authoring.md).
2. Claim a module in [issue #2](https://github.com/olserra/assistant-packs/issues/2).
3. Replace the `TODO` markers in that file and open a PR. One module per PR is perfect.

Every `TODO` in this folder is a real open task. Search for `TODO` to see them all.

## 🛡️ What this pack will never do

- Send an email, delete an email or unsubscribe without your explicit yes.
- Reply to anyone in your name automatically.
- Share the content of your mail outside your Instinct.
